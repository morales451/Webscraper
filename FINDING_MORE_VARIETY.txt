🔍 WHY YOU SEE THE SAME COMPANIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When you search "Roofing company in Houston 77002", Google shows:
  1. Big companies with lots of reviews
  2. Companies with wide service areas (serve multiple zip codes)
  3. Companies that rank well for generic terms

Result: You see "ABC Mega Roofing" in zip 77002, 77003, 77004, etc.

The deduplication prevents them from being added twice, but you're still
wasting time finding the same companies over and over.

================================================================================

✅ SOLUTION: USE MULTIPLE SEARCH QUERIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Instead of ONLY searching "Roofing company", also search for:

  🏢 SPECIALTY QUERIES (Find specialists - more likely Tier 1!)
     - "Roof coating specialist"
     - "Roof restoration service"
     - "Commercial roofing contractor"
     - "Industrial roofing contractor"
     - "Roof waterproofing"

  🔧 SERVICE TYPE QUERIES (Find smaller/niche companies)
     - "Flat roof contractor"
     - "Metal roofing contractor"
     - "TPO roofing contractor"
     - "Roof repair service"

  🎯 NICHE QUERIES (Find hidden gems)
     - "Emergency roof repair"
     - "Local roofer"
     - "Family owned roofing"
